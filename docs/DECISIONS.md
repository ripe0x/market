# Decision log

Status labels: Baseline means recommended product direction for implementation. Provisional means an engineering choice to validate and improve using evidence. Deferred means intentionally out of scope. Any change must include a date, evidence and consequence. Keep the Owner Review section current while working independently.

| D002 | Baseline | Seller chooses linear declining minimum NET WETH proceeds with optional nonzero floor, expiry and explicit zero floor opt in | Simple user control; clear safety constraint. Alternative gross price hidden fees was rejected. |

| D009 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | One Next.js app plus one keeper process sharing a SQLite file through Node's built-in `node:sqlite` | Zero database dependency. | D010 | Deferred | ERC20 swaps, cross chain, new token, full NFT marketplace and complex auctions | Distract from the validated seller problem and duplicate established systems. |
| D011 | Baseline | No authority for founder to take NFTs or seller proceeds; prefer a replaceable stateless version over upgradeable custody | Upgradeability should not create an extraction backdoor. |
| D012 | Baseline | Mainnet fork and actual signed Seaport orders required before claiming integration success | Mocks do not test real zones, signatures and consideration. |
| D013 | Baseline | Sellers may get a better price than their current minimum, but app cannot promise globally best price or guaranteed sale | Market data is incomplete and execution is competitive. |
| D014 | Superseded by D031 (2026-10-30) | Sellers are paid in WETH. Direct buyers pay ETH; Market wraps it. Overpayment returns as WETH | ETH payment removes a buyer approval transaction. See D017 for the refund token. |
| D015 | Superseded by D026 (2026-10-30) | Market contract: zone and sole fulfiller, no owner, no upgrade, no pause, fees as constants, one immutable protocol recipient | Options: native match only (fails surplus routing), custom EIP-712 authorization with NFT custody in our contract (new approval per seller, new signing scheme, custody during the tx, breaks for operator-restricted collections), Seaport zone (chosen). | D016 | Superseded by D030 (2026-10-30) | Price falls linearly from start to floor and the listing ends when it reaches the floor; no hold at floor | Seaport's native interpolation covers the whole order window. A hold would need either a second signed order or a custom price check that wallets would show as an opaque zone hash. Relisting is a free signature. Revisit if sellers ask for long floor holds. Owner review recommended. |
| D017 | Baseline | Direct-buy overpayment is returned as WETH, not ETH | Found on the fork: an ETH refund call into an EIP-7702 delegated buyer ran out of gas. A WETH transfer never runs buyer code, removes a reentrancy surface and a revert path. Cost: buyers may receive small WETH amounts. |
| D018 | Superseded by D026 (2026-10-30) | Listings use the OpenSea conduit key | Most sellers in established collections already approved it, so listing is usually signature-only; conduit transfers also suit operator-restricted collections. The approval is shared with OpenSea and outlives cancellation; the UI says so. Alternative: conduit key 0 (approve Seaport directly). |
| D019 | Superseded by D026 (2026-10-30) | Proceeds recipient is the seller | Simplest safe binding; the API rejects other recipients. Market itself pays surplus to whatever recipient the seller signed. |
| D020 | Revised by D034 (2026-10-30) | Supported offers: Seaport 1.6, single WETH offer item, ERC721 item offers and criteria-0 collection offers, WETH-only fee items. Trait offers excluded | Trait offers need proofs; non-WETH items would strand value. Market enforces the WETH offer; the keeper enforces the rest. |
| D021 | Provisional | OpenSea API key is required for matching and wallet NFT lists | Keyless responses are only served for cached URLs. Without a key the app still lists, buys, cancels and reads art from tokenURI; the footer shows matching off. |
| D022 | Baseline | Keeper submits one transaction at a time; a stuck one is replaced by a zero-value self transfer at the same nonce | OpenSea zone signatures expire in minutes, so re-sending old calldata is pointless. Simple nonce handling and idempotent restarts. |
| D023 | Baseline, owner approved (2026-10-30) | Shared escrow for all listings: the seller deposits the NFT into Market, and every listing sells from custody | OpenSea signs offers only for the NFT's owner (`PHASE0_EVIDENCE.md`). Owner decision 3: one shared escrow, no per-wallet contracts, independent cancellation and withdrawal, no administrator access. Fork evidence: `test/OpenSea.t.sol`. Residual: OpenSea may refuse Market's deployed address; only a mainnet canary proves it (Owner Review 6). |
| D024 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Settlement economics. The sale price is what the buyer pays. Royalty, protocol fee and executor fee come out of it; the remainder is the seller's proceeds. A listing's ask is the seller's minimum proceeds; a bid's price is the buyer's maximum payment | Owner decision 1. `buy` grosses the ask up to the smallest price whose proceeds cover it (two steps for a proportional royalty, four for others, within 4 wei of exact; fuzzed). Fees round down; built-in prices round toward the order's owner. `quoteBuy`, `quoteSell`, `quoteMatch` return the full split so integrators never rebuild it. Royalty changes and strategy changes can only make a fill revert: the buyer passes `maxPrice`, the seller's ask or `minProceeds` is checked atomically. |
| D025 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Surplus. `matchBid` fills at the bid's price, so the seller gets the surplus over the ask. `fillOffer` pays the seller everything the offer releases minus fees. `buy` charges only the gross-up of the ask. Arbitrage through other routes is allowed | Owner decision 2. Market guarantees authorized execution, not best execution. |
| D026 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | One Market contract holds every escrowed NFT and bid budget. Deposit is `safeTransferFrom(owner, market, id, data)` with the terms in `data`, made by the owner itself (`operator == from`). Users grant Market no NFT approvals. `cancelListing` and `withdrawListing` are separate, as are `cancelBid` and `withdrawBid`. Proceeds go to any recipient the seller names | Owner decision 3. One deposit transaction, no approval step, and an approved operator cannot set terms for an owner. Separate cancel means a failing NFT or token transfer cannot leave an order fillable. Alternatives: per-wallet escrow contracts (rejected by owner), approval plus `list()` (two transactions). Rollback: new deployment; sellers withdraw and relist. |
| D027 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Storage holds a hash of each order's terms plus `spent`, `fills` and `cancelled`. Full terms are emitted at creation and passed back on every call. No per-sale records or collection statistics; sales are `Sale` events plus the optional callback | Owner decision 4. One slot per listing instead of about nine (about 180k gas saved per listing). Fill-dependent pricing reads `fills`, which updates in the same call as the fill. Cost: callers must supply terms, which are in the creation event and transaction. Owner review: confirm events are enough for "verifiable sale records". |
| D028 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Collection callbacks: a collection that reports `ISaleObserver.onMarketSale` through ERC-165 is called after each sale with 100k gas, no value, under Market's reentrancy lock; failures are ignored. The caller must forward enough gas for the full allowance | Owner decision 5. The gas floor stops an executor from starving the callback on purpose. No retry, no hook registry. |
| D029 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Bids (acquisition programs) escrow their whole budget at creation. Terms: collection, token id range, currency, budget, `maxPrice` per NFT, quantity, recipient, expiry, price. A one-token range is an item bid; a full range is a collection bid | Owner decision 6. Escrowed funding means fills cannot fail for lack of funds or allowance and overlapping fills cannot overspend: `spent` and `fills` update atomically under the lock (invariant suite). Alternative: allowance-based bids, more capital efficient but Market would hold standing ERC20 approvals. Merkle or external eligibility deferred; ranges cover items, collections and Art Blocks style projects. |
| D030 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Pricing: built-in linear from `start` at `startTime` to `end` at `endTime`, holding the end value after (fixed, increasing, decreasing). Or an external `IPricingStrategy`, read with `staticcall` and 100k gas. Market clamps every answer: a listing never below `floor`; a bid never above `maxPrice` or the unspent budget | Owner decision 7. Strategies are permissionless and read-only and hold no custody. A failing strategy blocks fills only; withdrawal never calls it. External strategies may be mutable; the bounds are the guarantee. With escrow the price no longer has to end at the floor (D016): a listing holds its end price until expiry. |
| D031 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Currencies: WETH always enabled; the owner can enable other ERC20s. Deposits must arrive in full (fee-on-transfer rejected); tokens that return nothing are accepted. Disabling a currency stops new orders and fills; withdrawals continue. ETH is accepted for WETH listings and bids; excess and refunds return as WETH | Owner decision 8. Rebasing tokens would break escrow accounting and cannot be detected onchain, so they need the allowlist. D017's WETH refund is kept. |
| D032 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Royalties: ERC-2981 only, checked through ERC-165 and read with 50k gas; a malformed answer means no royalty; a royalty above the sale price makes the order unfillable. No Market royalty on OpenSea fills | Owner decision 8. The offer's own consideration already pays OpenSea's creator fee, so adding ERC-2981 there could charge twice (OpenSea's payout address may differ from the ERC-2981 receiver). Royalty Registry deferred: it is an upgradeable third-party proxy with multiple recipients. Owner review: optional-royalty collections get on OpenSea fills what OpenSea's offer pays. |
| D033 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Fees, constant per deployment: protocol 0.20% of the sale price on every sale; executor 0.30% to the caller of `matchBid` and `fillOffer`; referral 0.10% paid out of the protocol fee to a referrer named in `buy` or a sell into a bid | Owner decision 9. Users pay the same with or without a referrer. Protocol recipient is immutable. Review finding 2: anyone may name itself as referrer, so the protocol keeps 0.10% from self-referring users; a caller check would be bypassed by a second address. |
| D034 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | OpenSea route: `fillOffer` calls `Seaport.fulfillAdvancedOrder` with Market as fulfiller, for offers whose zone the owner enabled (OpenSea SignedZone at deploy). Market approves only the listed token and the exact currency consideration, and reverts if its balance falls. The keeper turns either OpenSea response shape into this call | Owner decision 10. Fork evidence: zone data from OpenSea's match-shaped fills works for direct fulfillment (both fixtures, `test/OpenSea.t.sol`), so one onchain route covers both shapes. The zone allowlist stops self-made offers that would skip royalties or run hostile zone code. Adversarial tests: an offer that spends escrow or names another NFT reverts. |
| D035 | Baseline (2026-10-30) | Owner role: enable currencies and zones, transfer the role. Nothing else | No pause, upgrade, fee change or asset access. A disabled currency or zone stops fills, never withdrawals. |

## Owner Review

Review these after the engineering agent has selected and implemented the simplest verified approach. The agent should record its final recommendation and alternatives without stopping to request ordinary approval.

1. D026: deposit by `safeTransferFrom` with terms, owner only; separate cancel and withdraw.
2. D027: no per-sale storage; sale records are events plus the optional callback. Confirm this meets "verifiable sale records".
3. D029: bids escrow their full budget (not allowance based). Eligibility is a token id range; merkle trait sets deferred.
4. D032: no Market royalty on OpenSea fills, to avoid double charging. Royalty Registry not used.
5. D033: 0.20% protocol, 0.30% executor on matches and OpenSea fills, 0.10% referral inside the protocol fee. Self-referral makes the protocol's take 0.10% on `buy` and bid sells (review finding 2). Alternatives: drop referral, or accept it as a rebate. Measured: OpenSea fills viable from about 0.052 WETH and bid matches from about 0.023 WETH at 0.4 gwei (`ECONOMICS.md`).
6. D023: mainnet canary still needed to prove OpenSea signs for Market's deployed address. Needs separate owner authorization and one low-value NFT.
7. Owner address (currencies and zones) and protocol fee recipient for deployment, and who controls each.
8. D014/D017: ETH in, WETH out for refunds. Confirm.
9. Review findings and launch blockers: `SECURITY.md`.
